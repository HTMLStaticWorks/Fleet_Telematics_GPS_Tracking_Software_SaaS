Our Mission & Purpose

Built to eliminate the chaos of modern fleet operations.

Commercial vehicle operators shouldn't have to juggle fragmented GPS tabs, paper inspection logs, disputed delivery times, and surprise maintenance breakdowns. FleetX was founded by logistics veterans and IoT engineers to give transport teams a single source of truth.

We transform raw telematics and CAN-bus data into clear, actionable intelligence so managers can protect their drivers, cut fuel waste, and deliver outstanding customer experiences on every route.

  • Sub-second telemetry: Ingest vehicle speed, location, and engine codes with zero lag.
  • Proactive safety culture: Real-time coaching that reduces harsh braking, speeding, and collisions.
  • Fuel transparency: Detect unauthorized idling, fuel siphoning, and route deviations instantly.
  • Zero unplanned downtime: Automated service alerts based on odometer and engine hours.
