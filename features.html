Fleet Analytics

Make operational patterns visible and actionable.

Fleet managers often struggle with massive spreadsheets across multiple depots. FleetX aggregates and translates complex telematics streams into clear executive scorecards.

  • Asset utilization: Identify underused vehicles and optimize vehicle distribution across depots.
  • Driver trend benchmarking: Reward top defensive drivers and schedule targeted coaching.
  • Total fuel economics: Track cost-per-kilometer across various routes, payloads, and driver shifts.
  • Preventative maintenance forecast: Predict part failures before they result in expensive towing fees.
