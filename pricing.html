Questions Answered

Frequently Asked Pricing Questions

Everything you need to know about contracts, hardware, and billing.

Are GPS tracking devices included or can I use my existing trackers?
FleetX is hardware-agnostic. You can either purchase our certified plug-and-play OBD-II or hardwired 4G LTE units, or connect your existing AIS-140/commercial GPS tracking devices via our universal telematics gateway.
Can I upgrade or downgrade plans as our vehicle count changes?
Yes! You can add new vehicles, pause decommissioned assets, or upgrade from Starter to Professional at any time through your dashboard administrator console with prorated billing.
Do you offer discounts for annual billing or larger fleet sizes?
We offer up to a 20% discount on annual commitments, as well as specialized volume discount tiers for commercial fleets operating more than 50 vehicles. Contact our sales team for an exact custom quote.
Is there a setup or onboarding fee?
Standard cloud onboarding and self-service training are completely free. For large enterprise fleets requiring nationwide on-site technician installation or custom ERP API middleware, our Professional Services team provides transparent one-time deployment packages.
Can we test FleetX on a pilot group before signing?
Absolutely. We offer a 14-day zero-obligation pilot evaluation on up to 5 vehicles so your dispatch and management teams can verify tracking accuracy, fuel savings, and scorecard analytics firsthand.

Need a tailored deployment proposal?

Share your vehicle count, tracking priorities, and integration requirements. Our telematics specialists will construct an ROI-backed pricing proposal within 24 hours.
