Service 01

Rapid Implementation & Hardware Onboarding

Deploying telematics shouldn't disrupt active transport schedules. Our certified technical teams handle end-to-end hardware installations — including OBD-II plug-ins, heavy-duty CAN-bus taps, fuel sensors, and dual-facing dashcams — during off-peak hours or depot downtime.

  • Zero vehicle downtime: Rapid installation scheduled around active delivery shifts.
  • Hardware pre-configuration: Every unit is SIM-activated, calibrated, and cloud-tested before installation.
  • Custom telemetry calibration: Fuel sensors, RPM limits, and speed thresholds configured per vehicle class.
  • Historical data migration: Seamlessly import your existing vehicle, driver, and depot records.
Service 02

Custom API & Enterprise System Integrations

Eliminate manual double-entry. Our integration engineering team builds bi-directional connectors between FleetX and your existing ERP (SAP, Oracle, NetSuite), Transport Management Systems (TMS), fuel card providers, and payroll software.

  • High-throughput REST & GraphQL: Real-time vehicle positions, odometer readings, and status sync.
  • Automated webhook pipelines: Trigger internal workflows on geofence arrival, maintenance flags, or accidents.
  • Fuel card reconciliation: Automatically match fuel pump transaction receipts with in-tank telemetry.
  • Driver payroll sync: Export verified operating hours, trip logs, and safety incentive bonuses.
