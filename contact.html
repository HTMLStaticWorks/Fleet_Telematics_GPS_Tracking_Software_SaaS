Frequently Asked Questions

What is included in the product demonstration?
Our product demonstration includes a full walkthrough of the FleetX platform tailored to your specific industry, an overview of hardware installation, and a Q&A session with our fleet experts.
How long does implementation take?
Most fleets can be fully onboarded within 2-4 weeks, depending on the size of your fleet and whether custom API integrations are required.
Do you offer custom pricing for large fleets?
Yes! For fleets with over 500 vehicles, we provide custom enterprise pricing tiers. Please reach out via the contact form above to get a tailored quote.
Can I integrate FleetX with my existing software?
Absolutely. FleetX offers a robust REST API and pre-built integrations with major ERP, payroll, and maintenance software systems.
What kind of support is available?
We provide 24/7 technical support for all our customers, along with dedicated account managers for enterprise tiers to ensure your fleet runs smoothly.
